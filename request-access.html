Straight answers

Before you request

What happens after I submit the request?

Someone on the Merik team reads it, creates your company workspace, and emails your admin login to the work email you gave. Requests are reviewed by a person, not approved automatically, so there is a short wait rather than an instant login.

Does it cost anything?

No. The workspace is free, with every module included and no limit on the number of employees. There is no setup fee and no card required. Guided setup around your existing data is the only paid option, and it is optional — see pricing.

How do employees get their logins?

You add each employee in the Employees module and create their login there. Employees then sign in to their own dashboard to mark attendance, request leave or work-from-home, log daily tasks and download their payslips.

Can I sign in right now without requesting?

Only if your company already has a Merik workspace. Employees use the email and password their admin created. Sign in at merik.in/app.
