Common questions

How do I get a Merik workspace for my company?

Use the request form at merik.in/request-access. A person on the Merik team reviews it, creates the workspace and emails your admin login. It is free, with no card and no setup fee.

I'm an employee and can't sign in. Who do I ask?

Your company's Merik admin creates employee logins, so ask them first. If you have a login and forgot the password, use "Forgot password?" on the sign-in screen at merik.in/app and a reset link is emailed to you.

Do you offer guided setup or a demo?

Yes. Guided setup — importing your departments, designations, CTC, holiday calendar, leave policy and asset registers — is the paid option on the pricing page. Email merik.msk@gmail.com with your team size and we will scope it with you.

How do I report a security issue?

Email merik.msk@gmail.com with "Security" in the subject line. Please do not post details publicly before we have had a chance to respond.
